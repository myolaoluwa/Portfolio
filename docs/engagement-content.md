# Engagement copy and commitment boundaries

The homepage engagement section describes a project-specific path from enquiry to delivery: discovery, scope/proposal, milestones, review, launch/handover, and support. It uses the DelighTech studio voice and preserves the separate design-approach section.

The FAQ explains what to discuss and agree in a proposal; it does not introduce a standard contract or invent commercial policies.

Not promised:

- A fixed delivery duration, immediate start, or guaranteed launch date.
- Free discovery, starting prices, a deposit percentage, or a specific billing model.
- Unlimited revisions or a fixed number of review rounds.
- Automatic ownership transfer or rights beyond third-party licence terms.
- A default warranty/support period, response SLA, 24/7 availability, or included hosting and maintenance.

Scope, pricing, payment terms, ownership/handover, milestones, review criteria, launch responsibilities, and support need project-specific written agreement. Any future standard terms should be supplied and approved by DelighTech before being published. This copy is an informational engagement overview, not legal advice or a substitute for an agreement.

Contact details remain placeholders at the user's request. This change adds no contact form, booking integration, paid service, or external publication. The FAQ uses native HTML disclosures so reading it does not require JavaScript and keyboard activation works without custom interaction code.
